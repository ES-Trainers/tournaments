If ES Trainers cancels a tournament

If the organizers cancel a tournament, every eligible paid registration receives a full refund of the registration fee it paid. "Eligible" means a confirmed registration whose payment was verified and has not already been refunded. The refund is for the exact amount paid, taken from the payment record, and goes back to the original payment method through Razorpay. You do not need to apply.

ES Trainers emails the team leader when a tournament is cancelled, with the amount, the refund ID and the refund status.

Refund initiated is not refund completed

A refund has two stages, and we tell you which one it is in:

  • Initiated: ES Trainers has sent the refund request to the payment provider.
  • Processed: the payment provider has confirmed the refund.

After that, the time your bank or card issuer takes to show the money is outside ES Trainers' control. We mark a refund as completed only after the payment provider reports it as processed. If an automatic refund fails, we keep the case as "manual refund required" and handle it by hand; the payment record and the refund status are kept for audit.

Failed, pending and unmatched payments

Registrations are confirmed only after server-side payment verification. If money is deducted but you do not see a confirmation, wait a minute and use "Retry confirming payment" on the registration page; do not pay again. If the payment really succeeded but the registration cannot be completed, the refund steps above apply. If you are unsure, email us with your payment ID.

Cancellation by a team

Slots are limited, so a team that withdraws after its registration is confirmed is not refunded automatically. We review requests that reach us before registration closes (48 hours before the tournament) and in special circumstances. This does not reduce any refund rights in the sections above or under the law.

Disqualification

A team disqualified for breaking the Fair Play Policy or tournament rules does not receive a refund of its registration fee. A team is never disqualified for something ES Trainers caused.

Rescheduling

If a tournament is moved to another date, existing registrations remain valid. If the new date cannot work for your team, contact support so we can review your request.

Refund help

Include your team name, tournament, registration ID or payment ID, and the leader email.

Contact: esportstraning@gmail.com. Our Contact page lists every support channel.
