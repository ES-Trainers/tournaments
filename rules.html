How registration works for every tournament

  • A BGMI team has four players; a fifth player is optional.
  • The team leader signs in with Google; the leader email must match the registration.
  • Within one tournament, each team name, leader email and leader phone number can be used for only one registration.
  • Registration closes exactly 48 hours before the tournament start (Indian Standard Time), or earlier if slots are full.
  • You accept the tournament rules before paying; a registration is confirmed only after the payment is verified by the server.
  • Room ID and password are shared with confirmed teams through official ES Trainers channels and must not be shared.

Cheating, disqualification and disputes

Cheating, exploits, unfair teaming, fake information and sharing room credentials can lead to disqualification. See the Fair Play Policy. Result disputes are reviewed by the organizers on evidence; see Result disputes.
