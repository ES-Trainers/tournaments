Who we are and what this covers

ES Trainers (estrainers.in) runs BGMI esports tournaments and team registration. This policy explains what personal information the registration and payment process collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. It describes how the site actually works today.

Information we collect

When a team leader registers a team, the registration form collects:

  • Team details: team name.
  • Players (four required, a fifth optional): each player's name, BGMI in-game name, BGMI UID and gender.
  • Team leader: email address and mobile phone number.
  • Google sign-in: to confirm the leader email you sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication. ES Trainers receives your verified Google email address; it never receives your Google password.
  • Rules acceptance: that the tournament rules were accepted, and when.
  • Payment references: Razorpay order ID, payment ID, amount, currency, payment and verification times, and, if a refund is made, the refund ID and status.
  • Registration records: registration ID, tournament, status, and email-delivery status.

What we do not collect: card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, net-banking credentials or bank passwords. Payment details are typed into Razorpay Checkout, which is operated by Razorpay; ES Trainers only receives the payment references listed above.

The website also uses Google Analytics to measure page visits (see "Cookies and analytics").

Why we use it

  • To create, verify and confirm your team's tournament registration.
  • To stop duplicate registrations: team names, leader emails and leader phone numbers must be unique within a tournament.
  • To verify payments, issue refunds, and keep payment records.
  • To send your registration confirmation email and, where needed, cancellation or refund notices.
  • To run the tournament, communicate with teams, and review fair-play or dispute cases.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent fraud or abuse.

Who processes your information

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore): stores registration records and verifies Google sign-in.
  • Google Apps Script and Gmail (MailApp): ES Trainers' server-side payment verification, registration saving and confirmation emails run here. Emails are sent from ES Trainers' Google account.
  • Razorpay: processes payments and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics: website usage measurement.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files and may log technical request data such as IP addresses.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with these service providers to operate the platform, with the tournament organizers who need it, or where the law requires it. Joining the WhatsApp Community or following Instagram is optional and is governed by those services' own policies.

What is visible to others

Registration records are not published on the website. Tournament pages show only tournament information and slot counts. If tournament results are published later, team names may appear in them. Player UIDs, phone numbers and emails are not published.

How long we keep it

We keep registration and payment records for as long as they are needed to run the tournament, handle refunds, complaints and disputes, and meet accounting and legal obligations. ES Trainers does not currently run automatic deletion. When a record is no longer needed we delete or anonymise it on request, unless we must keep it by law (for example payment records).

How we protect it

  • The website is served over HTTPS.
  • Payments are verified on the server: the payment signature, amount and currency are checked with Razorpay before a registration is confirmed. Your browser cannot confirm its own payment.
  • Registration records are written only by ES Trainers' server-side code, not by visitors' browsers, and database rules restrict who can read private records.
  • Phone and email uniqueness checks use stored identifiers, and the phone check is performed on the server.

No online system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If you believe your information was misused, contact us immediately.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can ask us to show you what we hold about your registration, correct it, or delete it. Email us from the leader email used for the registration so we can verify it is you, and include your team name and tournament. Deleting a record can cancel your tournament participation, and we may need to keep payment records as the law requires.

Contact: esportstraning@gmail.com. Our Contact page lists every support channel.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-9FHBM1T0L1), which may set cookies and collect usage information such as pages visited and approximate location. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or use a browser opt-out add-on. Registration itself needs no advertising cookies.

Minors

If you believe a player's information was submitted without the permission of a parent or guardian, contact us and we will review and, where appropriate, remove it.

Changes and contact

If we change how we handle information we will update this page and its date. Questions or privacy requests:
