Using ES Trainers

By registering a team or paying a registration fee on estrainers.in you agree to these terms, the Refund & Cancellation Policy, the Fair Play Policy, the Privacy Policy and the tournament rules published for the tournament you enter. ES Trainers currently organizes BGMI tournaments only.

Eligibility

  • You must be able to enter into a binding agreement under applicable law. Players who are minors should take part only with the permission of a parent or guardian, who is then responsible for the registration.
  • Players must be eligible to play BGMI under the game's own terms and rules.
  • The team leader must have a Google account whose email matches the leader email on the registration.

Registration requirements

  • A team has four players; a fifth player is optional.
  • Every player's name, in-game name, BGMI UID and gender must be real and accurate. A UID cannot be repeated within a team.
  • Within one tournament, each team name, each leader email and each leader phone number can be used for only one registration. The same phone number may be used again in a different tournament.
  • You must accept the rules published for the tournament before paying.
  • Providing false information can lead to disqualification (see Fair Play Policy).

Registration fees and payment

  • Registration fees can differ from one tournament to another. The fee shown on the tournament page is the fee for that tournament, and the amount charged is set by ES Trainers on the server, not by your browser.
  • Payments are made through Razorpay. A registration is confirmed only after ES Trainers' server has verified the payment. Closing the payment window is not proof of payment.
  • If your payment succeeds but the registration cannot be completed (for example the team name or phone number was taken first, the tournament filled or closed, or the fee changed while you paid), ES Trainers refunds the payment as described in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • If the fee for a tournament is changed, payments already completed keep the amount that was paid.

Registration deadline and slots

Registration closes exactly 48 hours before the tournament start time (Indian Standard Time), or earlier if all slots are filled or the tournament status is changed. The deadline is enforced by the server. Slots are limited and are given in order of confirmed registration.

Tournament participation and match rules

  • Each tournament has its own details and rules, published on its tournament page and on the Tournament Rules page. Where a rule differs between tournaments, the rule published for that tournament applies.
  • Teams must be ready at the time announced for the tournament. A team that is absent or late may lose its slot without a refund unless the delay was caused by ES Trainers.
  • Match results are decided by the organizers using the in-game results and the published scoring system.

Room ID and password

Match room details are shared only with confirmed teams through official ES Trainers channels. Do not share them with anyone outside your registered team. Sharing room details, or joining with a player who is not on your registration, can lead to disqualification.

Prize pools

  • Prize pools are funded from tournament registration (entry) fees. The prize pool for each tournament is set by ES Trainers and shown on the tournament page.
  • The prize pool is not automatically calculated from the number of registered teams unless the tournament page says so.
  • Winner-wise prize amounts, extra rewards and conditions are shown only when ES Trainers has published them. Until then the tournament page shows them as not published.
  • Prizes are not guaranteed to any team. They are awarded according to the published results and conditions, to the team registered in the winning entry.

Disqualification

Teams that break the Fair Play Policy or the tournament rules can be warned, penalized, or disqualified, and lose prizes. Disqualification for a rule violation does not entitle a team to a refund of its registration fee. A team will not be disqualified, and will be refunded, for problems that ES Trainers caused.

Changes, rescheduling and cancellation by ES Trainers

ES Trainers may change a tournament's schedule or details, or cancel it. If ES Trainers cancels a tournament, every eligible paid registration is refunded the full registration fee that was paid. Details and what "refund initiated" means are in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Disputes and complaints

Raise any registration, payment or result dispute by email, with your team name, tournament, registration ID or payment ID, and any evidence (screenshots or recordings). ES Trainers will review it and reply using the email on the registration. Organizer decisions on in-game results are made on the evidence available.

Contact: esportstraning@gmail.com. Our Contact page lists every support channel.

Using the platform

Do not submit fake registrations, attempt to bypass payment or uniqueness checks, interfere with the website, or use another person's identity. We can cancel registrations created this way; payments are handled as described in the refund policy.

Responsibility

ES Trainers organizes tournaments with reasonable care, but is not responsible for BGMI game servers or updates, internet or device problems on the player's side, or outages of third-party services such as Razorpay or Google. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under law that cannot be limited, including your right to a refund as set out in our refund policy.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India.

Support and communication

We communicate by email to the leader email, and through the official WhatsApp Community and website. Make sure the leader email is one you check.

Contact: esportstraning@gmail.com. Our Contact page lists every support channel.
