Who we are

ES Trainers is an esports tournament and community platform focused on BGMI and Free Fire MAX.

We organize competitive tournaments for squads in both games and publish the details of each tournament in one place, so teams can see what is coming up and join in.

BGMI and FF MAX each have their own tournament page on this website.

What we do

The activities that make up ES Trainers today.

BGMI tournaments

Competitive BGMI tournaments, with squad matches played across multiple maps.

FF MAX tournaments

Fast-paced squad tournaments built for Free Fire MAX.

Tournament information

The match, entry fee, prize pool and team slots are published for every tournament.

Upcoming events

Teams can see upcoming and live tournaments on the BGMI and FF MAX pages.

Registration pathways

When registration is open, the tournament links straight to its team registration page.

A gaming community

Announcements and updates are shared with players on WhatsApp and Instagram.

Our focus and community

ES Trainers is built around competitive play and the people who take part in it.

  • Competitive gaming

    Tournaments are the centre of ES Trainers: teams play structured matches against each other.

  • Organized tournaments

    Each tournament is listed with its match, entry fee, prize pool and slot count.

  • Team participation

    Tournaments are played by squads, so registration is made for teams rather than individuals.

  • Community engagement

    Players can join the WhatsApp community and follow ES Trainers on Instagram.

  • Clear tournament information

    Details are shown as published, and slot counts update on the page without a refresh.

  • Accessible participation

    The site is built for mobile, with a simple path from a tournament to its registration page.
