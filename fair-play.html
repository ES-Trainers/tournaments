Fair Play Policy

What is not allowed in ES Trainers BGMI tournaments, and how reports and disqualifications are handled.

On this page

  1. Our commitment
  2. What is not allowed
  3. How reports and reviews work
  4. Possible outcomes
  5. Result disputes

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Our commitment

ES Trainers tournaments are meant to be won by skill. This policy lists what is not allowed and how cases are handled. ES Trainers does not claim to run an automated anti-cheat system: reviews are done by the organizers using in-game results, reports and evidence.

What is not allowed

  • Hacking and cheats: any third-party software, modified game files or tools that give an unfair advantage.
  • Exploits: deliberately using game bugs or glitches for advantage.
  • Unfair teaming: working with, or sharing information with, other teams in a match, or deliberately feeding another team.
  • Match manipulation: fixing, throwing or arranging match results.
  • Abusive behaviour: harassment, hate speech or threats toward players, organizers or community members.
  • Fake information: false player names, UIDs, other people's identities, or a player who is not on the registration.
  • Room credential sharing: sharing room ID or password outside the registered team.
  • Fake or duplicate registrations to take extra slots or bypass uniqueness checks.

How reports and reviews work

  1. Report a concern as soon as possible after the match by emailing us, with your team name, tournament, the match and any evidence (screenshots, recordings, in-game IDs).
  2. The organizers review the evidence, and may ask the teams involved for more information or recordings.
  3. The organizers decide. Reports without evidence may not be actionable.
  4. We reply to the leader email on the registration.

Contact: esportstraning@gmail.com. Our Contact page lists every support channel.

Possible outcomes

Depending on how serious the violation is: a warning, point deductions, removal of a match result, disqualification, loss of prizes, and refusal of future registrations. A team disqualified for a violation is not refunded its registration fee. See Refund & Cancellation and Terms.

Result disputes

If you think a result or point total is wrong, send the dispute with evidence as soon as the result is announced. Organizers review it against the published scoring and in-game results, and correct the result if an error is found.
